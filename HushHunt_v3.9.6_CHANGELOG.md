# Hush Hunt v3.9.6

## What changed
- Refined cat thumbnails across all cat kinds with extra fur markings and more natural face detail.
- Refined dog artwork for poodle, terrier, spaniel, bulldog, dachshund, husky, golden retriever and mixed breed.
- Improved realism/detail on garden and grove snail.
- Added a broader realism/detail pass across butterflies, moths, bees, ladybirds, beetles, ants, spiders and birds (except swan/owl were largely retained).
- Reworked flying squirrel and other squirrel artwork.
- Increased distinction between Netherland Dwarf and Flemish Giant rabbits.
- Differentiated common frog, marsh frog, bullfrog and other frog.
- Added safer framing/padding for slug and other animal thumbnails that previously looked cropped.
- ARCore source update: plane finding now explicitly enables horizontal and vertical plane detection.

## Notes
- This package updates source assets and ARCore source code.
- Android device deployment is still required to verify ARCore placement behaviour on the target phone.
