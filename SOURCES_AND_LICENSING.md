# Wildlife source guide (review-first)

**Eight direct model download entries** are retained from the earlier Hush Hunt importer, downloaded solely to the owner's Windows PC: red panda, plains zebra, grey wolf, red fox, red kangaroo, hippopotamus, Bengal tiger and African elephant. Their source pages are in `WILDLIFE_61_SLOTS.json`.

Other promising **research-only** listings (no automatic downloads or redistribution):

- **Silverback gorilla:** [Sketchfab: Free Gorilla](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-gorilla-ad28003205884841a028e80edb4089ef) — CC BY claimed on listing; verify original author and downloaded licence, provide attribution.
- **Red panda:** [Fab: Lesser Red Panda](https://www.fab.com/listings/7088dc2e-ee62-45b1-a5ef-a29d65f4120e) — commercial listing, rigged/animated claims require testing, purchase/licence required.
- **Plains zebra:** [Sketchfab: Zebra Rigged](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/zebra-rigged-5d6ff2749afc438b9989f91ecb242574) — CC BY; manually inspect and download with login.
- **Mixed professional fauna:** [Fab: Animalia Starter Kit](https://www.fab.com/listings/2c7944a6-8b49-4c0b-9ad1-108e1637a8a9), [Fab: Animal Pack Ultra](https://www.fab.com/listings/a29a8f63-d025-4a00-ab86-75b05667671b) — engine-only formats may require permitted conversion, licensing and mobile optimisation.
- **General animal catalogues:** [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com), [Fab](https://www.fab.com), [CGTrader](https://www.cgtrader.com), [1DesignTool animals](https://1designtool.com/3d/collections/animals).

**Licensing:** Sketchfab authenticated downloads require checking each model's Creative Commons licence and giving attribution where required. CGTrader's royalty-free licence generally allows incorporation in an app but restricts extraction and raw-asset redistribution. Fab likewise has its own model-specific conditions. 1DesignTool states commercial app use is allowed but standalone model packs cannot be republished. Confirm terms before installation and public release.

**Quality:** More triangles ≠ more realism. For every animal, inspect both eyes, correct ears/tails, attached paws, anatomical silhouette, alpha-blended fur, 2K textures, appropriate physical scale, surface position, mobile frame rate and actual rigging for Walk/Pet. A static GLB will otherwise glide.
